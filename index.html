    Reading the charts

    Excellent, Good, Sufficient and Poor use the Bathing Water Regulations 2013 limits and colours, as summarised by Surfers Against Sewage. Inland E. coli: Excellent under 500, Good under 900, Sufficient under 1,000, otherwise Poor; enterococci 200 / 330 / 400. Coastal and transitional E. coli: 250 / 500 / 500 (so no Sufficient band); enterococci 100 / 185 / 200 cfu/100 ml. Officially, Excellent and Good are 95th percentiles and Sufficient a 90th percentile over four seasons, which is why the Sufficient limit sits below the Good limit. Here each single sample is placed in the band it would fall in, so the colours are a guide, not a classification. A zero bacteria count is plotted at 1 on log scales. Distance to an overflow ignores whether it is upstream or downstream of a site. Some readings in the source file look like entry errors (for example a temperature of 136); they are shown as recorded.
